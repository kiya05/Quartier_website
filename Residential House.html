QUARTIER STUDIO · INTERIOR DESIGN

Residential House, Sector 22

01
PROJECT INFO

An Interior With Its Own Identity Own Identity.

Our team has designed a complete furnished residential interior development in Sector 22 Noida that extends from the ground level through third-level areas and includes the rooftop space. The Quartier Studio team reworked every part of the space to merge luxury with practicality.

This multi-level house with furniture on all floors showcases an elegant layout space for living and sleeping areas and features a kitchen design system and a terraced garden.

Every detail has been developed to reflect the personality of the client while maintaining Quartier Studio's timeless design language.

CLIENT Cameron Williamson
LOCATION Gurugram, India
CATEGORY Interior Design
DESIGNER Deep Prabha
3D DESIGNER Zishan
PROJECT MANAGER Asif Saifi
02 · LUXURIOUS LIVING SPACES

Luxury, Reimagined for Everyday Life.

The living spaces were designed to feel expansive, warm and effortlessly sophisticated.

Natural materials, custom furniture, soft textures and architectural lighting create a calm visual rhythm throughout the residence.

Luxury is expressed through proportion, materiality and attention to detail rather than excess.
