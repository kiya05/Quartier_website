Ideal Bathroom Placement According to Vastu Shastra

The placement and orientation of a bathroom are important considerations for homeowners who follow Vastu Shastra principles. Proper planning can help create a bathroom that feels balanced, comfortable, and harmonious with the overall layout of the home.

According to traditional Vastu guidelines:

Key Principles

  • Northwest direction: The northwest corner is traditionally considered one of the preferred locations for a bathroom.
  • West and North directions: These directions are also commonly considered suitable for bathroom placement, depending on the overall floor plan.
  • Northeast direction: The northeast is traditionally avoided for bathrooms because it is considered an important zone for maintaining a sense of positive energy and openness.
  • Centre of the home: The central area of the house is generally recommended to remain open and free from bathroom placement according to traditional Vastu principles.
