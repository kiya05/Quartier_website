Particle Board by Quartier Studio – A Budget-Friendly Choice Recommended by Interior Design Company in Noida

Particle Board is an economical furniture board that is produced by gluing and compressing wood particles with resin.

It is light in weight and is generally applied in mass-produced, low-priced furniture. It is utilized for temporary furniture or even areas with less moisture.

Beds made of particle board are available in a variety of brands, such as Greenpanel, Action TESA and Archidply, which offer satisfactory quality of particle board for budget interiors.

The 5 points to consider are as follows:
Budget-Friendly Option
  • Particle board is one of the cheapest furniture boards available in the market.
  • It’s a great choice for those seeking affordable furniture for temporary rental properties or short-term accommodations.

  • Easy to move and easy to install because of its light weight.

Toilet Seat & Fixture Placement

The placement of bathroom fixtures plays an important role in creating an efficient and comfortable interior. From the toilet seat and washbasin to the shower and mirror, every element should be positioned according to the available space, plumbing requirements and the homeowner's design preferences.

VASTU PRINCIPLES
  • The toilet seat is traditionally positioned so the user faces north or south.
  • Shower and washbasin areas can be planned toward the east, north or northeast.
  • Mirrors are traditionally preferred on the north or east wall.
  • Fixture placement should maintain adequate circulation and comfortable usability.

Ventilation & Natural Light

Good ventilation and natural light are essential for a healthy and comfortable bathroom. Proper airflow helps control humidity, condensation and unpleasant odours while natural light can make the bathroom feel brighter and more spacious.

DESIGN PRINCIPLES
  • Provide windows wherever the architectural layout allows.
  • East and north-facing windows are traditionally preferred in Vastu-inspired planning.
  • Use an exhaust fan where natural ventilation is insufficient.
  • Combine natural and artificial lighting for better functionality.

Bathroom Drainage & Water Flow

Efficient drainage is an essential part of every bathroom design. Proper floor gradients and strategically positioned drainage outlets help prevent water accumulation and make the bathroom easier to maintain.

DESIGN PRINCIPLES
  • Drainage should allow water to flow efficiently without stagnation.
  • Traditional Vastu guidelines often favour east, north or northeast drainage directions.
  • Proper floor slope is essential for effective water movement.
  • Plumbing should comply with applicable construction and building requirements.

Colours for a Vastu-Inspired Bathroom

Colour plays an important role in the visual character of a bathroom. Soft and neutral tones can create a calm atmosphere while maintaining the clean and sophisticated appearance expected from a contemporary interior.

COLOUR PRINCIPLES
  • Prefer soft, neutral and naturally inspired shades for a calm visual environment.
  • Use white, beige and light earthy tones for a clean contemporary appearance.
  • Introduce darker colours through controlled accents rather than overwhelming the space.
  • Coordinate wall, flooring and fixture colours for a cohesive interior.
